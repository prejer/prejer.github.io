OrgoMap

A Jira app that gives managers a bird's-eye view of the whole organisation in one click. Every task is a shape — colour shows its status, length shows how long it has been stuck there — with near-real-time data via webhooks. Instead of endless tables and late reports, OrgoMap flags problems with a clear message and lets you act on them right away.
