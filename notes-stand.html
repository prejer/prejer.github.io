Desk note stand

A desk stand for reusable notes: magnetic sheets with a dry-erase coating that you write on, wipe and stick back on the stand. My second attempt at bringing a physical product to market — I modelled the parts in Fusion 360, 3D-printed them, combined them with a steel post and felt-covered arms, and prepared materials and packaging for a small batch.
