Slabaxis

My first B2C SaaS: a web platform for building simple electronics without designing a PCB. You arranged the components in the browser, and the platform generated a 3D model of a mounting board — print it, insert the parts, and you had a ready "pseudo-PCB" with labels and graphics built in. The project is now closed; I'm no longer developing it.

Technical overview

How it works

From an idea to a working circuit without ordering a PCB or soldering on perfboard.

Arrange

Pick components from the library and place them on the board in the browser editor; add text and a logo.

Generate

The platform generates a 3D model of the board with holes and seats for every component, ready to export.

Print & build

Print the board, insert the parts and connect them — a finished device with its graphics already on it.
